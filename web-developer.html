Full-Stack Web Developer

OptiFlow Systems is a top software company focused on creating innovative solutions to improve business processes and boost productivity. Our passionate team is dedicated to delivering outstanding products and services. Join us to be part of a company where creativity and technology drive success.

About the Role


We are looking for Full Stack Web Developers to join our team and work on our main product. We aim for excellence and use the latest tools and technologies in a challenging business environment.

If you are passionate about programming and have great skills, we would love to discuss some fantastic opportunities at our Lahore office with you.

Once you join, you will have the chance to work with some of the brightest and most talented professionals in the industry.


Job Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain web applications using front-end and back-end technologies.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define project requirements and deliver high-quality solutions.
  • Ensure the responsiveness and performance of web applications.
  • Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code.
  • Troubleshoot, debug, and upgrade existing software.
  • Implement security and data protection measures.
  • Stay updated with industry trends and emerging technologies.
  • Develop documentation throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

Job Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 3 years of experience in full stack web development.
  • Proven experience with front-end frameworks (e.g., React, Angular, Vue.js) and back-end technologies (e.g., Node.js, Django, Ruby).
  • Strong knowledge of server-side programming languages and frameworks.
  • Experience with databases (SQL, NoSQL) and RESTful APIs.
  • Applicants should be proficient with Code Repository tools.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple projects.

What We Offer

  • A market competitive salary and package.
  • A diverse team and inclusive culture.
  • A focus on individual learning and development.
  • Group life insurance.
  • Medical In-Patient, Out-Patient (Self, Spouse, Children & Parents).
  • In-house gym, Hospital, company meal.
  • All expense paid International Business Travel plus travel allowances.
  • Paid Annual Leaves, Sick Leaves, Hajj Leaves, Maternity Leaves.
